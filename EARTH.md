# Earth comparison dataset

Run `python build_earth.py`, then `python -m pip install rasterio==1.5.2` and `python add_earth_radar.py`. Outputs are local in `outputs/earth-comparison.sqlite`. The default enrichment budgets select the 12 densest eligible NASADEM tiles per survey, then the eight densest climate grid points per survey among enriched terrain sites. Site and chemistry catalogs are ingested independent of those budgets.

## Source records and GIS

- **GEMAS**, BGR / EuroGeoSurveys, [extended release](https://data.europa.eu/data/datasets/0554568e-c60f-4106-b099-839bb30f9c44?locale=en). Use the measured WD-XRF oxide tables joined to identification/coordinates with their source sample IDs. Six positive complete features in weight percent; prior audit checked the detection limits. The two groups represent arable soil at 0–20 cm and grazing soil at 0–10 cm. See [feature definitions](CODEBOOK.md). The input ZIP is hash-pinned.
- **NGSA**, Geoscience Australia, [de Caritat and Cooper](https://doi.org/10.11636/Record.2011.020). Use non-QC-duplicate coarse (<2 mm) upper outlet sediments (0–10 cm), six total XRF measurements at or above the column detection thresholds, excluding missing/censored values. Preserve elemental mg/kg, source columns and line. Multiply by oxide formula mass / constituent elemental mass and divide by 10,000 to express oxide-equivalent wt%. This conversion gives a reporting basis and does not identify mineral phases. NGSA sediment is a distinct sample medium. Keep collection date and original GDA94 coordinates. Position uses the GDA94 coordinates as approximate WGS84 for regional GIS only. The file identifies CC BY 3.0 Australia; the catalogue currently also lists CC BY 4.0. Retain Geoscience Australia attribution.
- **NASA NASADEM HGT v001**, [NASA guide](https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/2237/NASADEM_User_Guide_V13.pdf), COGs from [OpenTopography](https://portal.opentopography.org/raster?jobId=rt1792450312004). GIS uses a spherical local approximation with radius 6,371,008.8 m. Bin the source postings in a centered 5 km square onto a 10×10 grid with 500 m spacing. Require 95% valid pixels in each bin. Run the exact shared planetary `terrain_metrics` calculation at 1,000 m nominal central-difference baseline. Compare at regional scale. Source products can differ in effective resolution, vegetation response, and vertical reference. Skip windows crossing the selected tile boundary until mosaicking is available. Record tile URL, SHA-256, pixel ranges, and bin counts.
- **NASA POWER Hourly API**, [documentation](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/hourly/), MERRA-2. This version uses NASA POWER in place of ERA5-Land. Retrieve January, April, July and October 15, 2024 UTC. Keep hourly air temperature at 2 m, modeled skin temperature, and surface pressure from the same hour. Convert Celsius to Kelvin and kPa to Pa. GIS joins the site to the nominal 0.5°×0.625° request grid and records great-circle distance to that request point. The product is a coarse regional estimate, not a station at the sample. Lunar and Martian brightness temperatures and Earth modeled skin temperature are distinct products. Pressure values are site pressure, and the planetary pressure gap matters physically.
- **RADARSAT-1**, CSA / NRCan, [public COG archive](https://registry.opendata.aws/radarsat-1/), Open Government Licence – Canada. Two identified scenes are pinned by object key. GIS transforms site coordinates into scene pixel space using the embedded ground control points and a thin-plate spline. Take the median of positive valid pixels in a 21×21 pixel window with at least 400 usable pixels. Hash the sampled valid values; retain scene UTC metadata, URL, row and column. Values are raw digital numbers. These are historical single-scene context, without a calibrated before/after moisture inference or any join to 2024 weather.
- **MODIS MOD11A1.061**, [NASA collection](https://doi.org/10.5067/MODIS/MOD11A1.061). CMR metadata returned a candidate granule, G2182591980-LPCLOUD. This release has no MODIS pixel values.

`sites` carries the GIS ID and WGS84 footprint, `samples` has measured chemistry and provenance, `terrain` has five regional metrics, `weather` has unique grid-hour scenarios with hashes and `site_weather` records GIS links. `joined_inputs` contains only rows with all three core layers. `radar` is an optional contextual table. The snapshot has no records from North America, Africa or Asia. There are no simulated measurements, route claims, gap filling, or claims of cross-instrument calibration.

Verified snapshot: 5,345 chemistry samples at 5,240 sites, 179 terrain profiles, 1,536 unique weather windows, 52 sites with all core layers, and 16 RADARSAT site observations across 2 scenes. MODIS asset retrieval returned HTTP 401 without Earthdata authorization.
