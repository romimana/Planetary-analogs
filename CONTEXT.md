# Planetary sample context

Build the Mars and Moon databases, run `python curate_references.py`, then run `python build_context.py`. Open `outputs/Planetary Context.html` or inspect `outputs/planetary-context.sqlite`. The build caches public source inputs under `work/context/`. Raster byte ranges are validated on download and their SHA-256 digests are in the profile. The downloadable rover route may be revised by its publisher. The export records the exact waypoint used for each association.

## Chemistry and location

The export retains the 149 Mars records and 12 Apollo records from the soil [codebook](CODEBOOK.md). The 12 Mars records flagged by the chemistry screen remain flagged. Every row includes measured concentrations, method context or source, and the original row locator.

- Gale positions come from the APXS easting/northing in the archive. Inverse simple cylindrical projection uses the [JPL PLACES SIS](https://planetarydata.jpl.nasa.gov/w10n/msl/msl_places/document/PLACES_PDS_SIS.pdf) and Mars radius 3396190 m. The build independently checks the transform against NASA waypoint geographic and projected coordinates.
- Apollo 17 samples 72501, 76501 and 78221 use station 2, 6 and 8 rover parking positions respectively. [Haase et al.](https://doi.org/10.1029/2018EA000408) gives ME coordinates in tables 6, 10 and 12. Soil and station identifications come from the linked Lunar Sample Atlas and catalog entries. The station is a proxy for the collection point.
- Other Apollo records and the Spirit/Opportunity records use mission anchors. Terrain and environmental statistics at those anchors describe the mission area. Spatial precision at the sample is unverified.

## Terrain

Use the native [MOLA 128 ppd](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/mgs/urn-nasa-pds-mgs_mola_topography_derived/meg128/) and [SLDEM2015 512 ppd](https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/54) numeric data. Extract cell centers inside a local 5×5 km rectangle. Pixel spacings use body radius and the cosine of center latitude. Compute median and p90 gradient slope, fraction over 10°, p90–p10 relief, and RMS residual relative to the four direct neighbors. Terrain slope and residual depend on resolution. Native postings are roughly 460 m on Mars and 59 m in lunar north–south direction. SLDEM2015 values are stored in kilometers and converted to meters. For harmonized regional comparison, average source cell centers into a 10×10 grid of 500 m cells and repeat the metrics with a 1,000 m central difference baseline. The export includes the minimum and maximum source counts per aggregate cell. Effective source resolution still differs. These regional statistics cannot resolve rover-scale obstacles. Soil type is carried only where supported by the chemistry/collection source. Generic material labels do not imply a mapped soil unit or measured engineering texture.

## Environmental windows and distance

- **Mars.** Use calibrated [REMS MODRDR](https://pds.nasa.gov/ds-view/pds/viewProfile.jsp?dsid=MSL-M-REMS-5-MODRDR-V1.0) on selected sols. Aggregate valid reads into local true solar time hours and retain counts, median, minima and maxima per sensor. Use the rightmost two ambient-temperature quality indicators and the pressure shadow indicator. Require all four surface brightness indicators to be good. Range filters exclude invalid values. A retained hour has air temperature and pressure, and ground brightness where the stricter quality rule leaves data. These are summaries over available reads per sensor within an hour. The hourly values need not be simultaneous.
- The position proxy is the latest documented endpoint before the environmental sol. Sols with a published endpoint on that same sol are skipped because the rover's position can change during the hour. Associations are within 1 km of the proxy; this is a project rule and does not certify location stability. Retain the chemistry-to-environment sol offset. Stationary and moving rover uncertainty should be checked in the operations record before high-precision use. Spirit/Opportunity atmospheric windows were not assigned.
- **Moon.** Use [Diviner GCP bolometric brightness temperature](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2016.08.012), covering 2009–2015. Extract the 96 quarter-hour bins in the containing 0.5° cell and retain positive valid temperatures. These are cumulative orbital surface measurements grouped by local solar hour, with spatial support about 15 km north–south. They do not represent Apollo-era readings or a modern field forecast.
- Great-circle distances use a custom body radius (Mars 3396190 m; Moon 1737400 m), measured between the profile point and the relevant rover waypoint or lunar bin center. The Moon field is not the observation footprint radius. No Earth data enter this build.
