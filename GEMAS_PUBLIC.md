# Public European chemistry

The explorer bundles measurements from the [Geological Survey Ireland GEMAS release](https://data.gov.ie/dataset/gsi-gemas-european-geochemical-data), distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The BGR extended download used in the earlier local work is a different distribution. [BGR metadata](https://umweltportal.niedersachsen.de/suche/detail?id=numis-metadaten-0554568e-c60f-4106-b099-839bb30f9c44) links to general terms whose Article 3 limits public republication absent applicable overrides or permission. The public package imports measurement values and coordinates only from the GSI distribution.

## Data lineage and checks

- Read the [GSI ArcGIS feature service](https://gsi.geodata.gov.ie/server/rest/services/Geochemistry/IE_GSI_GEMAS_Geochemistry_Agricultural_Grazing_Land_Soil_EU_WGS84/MapServer), layer 190 for arable soil at 0–20 cm and layer 228 for grazing soil at 0–10 cm. Their XRF attributes report six oxide mass percentages. Raw JSON results are pinned by SHA-256. Each bundled sample records the query URL, the digest and its ArcGIS object ID.
- Filter on the source sample group and integral sample ID. Check coverage and response CRS, all six positive values, published lower detection limits, and a plausible sum. Compare by group and sample ID against the local extended release. Allow absolute reporting differences of 0.06 wt% for silica, magnesium oxide and calcium oxide, 0.006 wt% for titanium dioxide and potassium oxide, and 0.0011 wt% for manganese oxide. Exclude the 12 agricultural samples that disagree beyond these tolerances. Their IDs are in the builder. This check uses the local release for verification only.
- GIS joins the rounded, four-decimal WGS84 survey coordinates to [Natural Earth Admin 0](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/50m-admin-0-countries-2/) and GLiM. Terrain uses the same 5 km NASADEM window and 500 m bins as the USGS and Tellus snapshots. The published build reused archived NASADEM-derived windows only at exact four-decimal coordinates. Three source windows were independently replayed with exact raster hashes, identical posting counts and matching features. The Earth solar scenarios use the same 24 SPICE reference times as the main database.
- The two published layers returned 4,137 features. After chemistry screening, 4,119 records passed. The final [SQLite snapshot](data/gemas-public.sqlite) contains 3,503 sample records at 3,409 sites with validated terrain, including 3,294 records with mapped geology. This includes 400 sample records in Spain and 155 in Greece. [Coverage and checksum](data/gemas-public.json).

Run `python build_gemas_public.py` to calculate terrain directly from NASADEM with the Earth expansion dependencies. The `--terrain-cache` option uses the existing `outputs/terrain-local.json` raster derivatives to reproduce the published build. Rebuild the app with `python build_app.py`, `node app/recommend_samples.js`, and `python build_app.py` again.

## British Geological Survey

[BGS Geology 625K](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/bgs-geology-625k/) is available under the Open Government Licence and can add UK bedrock context. The [UK soil geochemistry](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/uk-topsoil-geochemistry/) and [stream sediment geochemistry](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/uk-stream-sediment-geochemistry/) pages distinguish open grids from licensed survey points. A [south-west England release](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/g-base-for-south-west-england/) provides open modeled grids, with a different panel and spatial support from the measured GEMAS samples. The explorer uses the GSI measured data for this expansion.
