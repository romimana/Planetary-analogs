# Analog matching pilot

Run `python build_pilot.py` after building both reference databases. Open `outputs/Analog Pilot.html` locally. The page ranks measurements offline and links to candidate coordinates. `outputs/analog-pilot.sqlite` contains the six fields, normalization scales, source locators and every eligible record. The UI offers equal weights, a 4× priority shortcut, explicit weights, and single-variable ranking.

Earth data are the Ap and Gr WD-XRF oxide tables from the 2021 GEMAS extended release by BGR and EuroGeoSurveys, joined by sample identifier within the same sample group to the published WGS84 coordinates. The ZIP is pinned by SHA-256. Use only complete rows with positive finite concentrations and valid regional coordinates. Ap covers 0–20 cm agricultural soil and Gr covers 0–10 cm grazing soil. Earth data are linked to BGR's archive and its terms. Outputs and source ZIP stay local.

The reference selections are four Mars areas/subareas from three missions and four Moon mission areas. All chosen measurements describe soil or regolith. Ap and Gr samples and planetary measurements differ in preparation and spatial support. This is a chemistry screening score, with no cross-instrument calibration claim. Bulk lunar records come from Lunar Sourcebook table 7.16. Preserve preparation codes and source locators.

For each field use weight percent as reported. Divide the Earth–target difference by that field's Earth interquartile range (75th minus 25th percentile) and calculate the square root of the weighted mean squared differences. All eligible Earth rows have the same six dimensions. The result is unitless. The IQR is an empirical scaling factor, not a scientific equivalence threshold. Near matches are relative to the GEMAS candidates and must still pass field and instrument validation. Single-variable ties are ordered by sample ID. Terrain and weather are visibly disabled until matching Earth and planetary fields are loaded.
