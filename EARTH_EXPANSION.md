# Earth expansion for the analog explorer

The browser app reads the original public snapshot and a separate `data/earth-expansion.sqlite` snapshot. The [coverage report](data/earth-expansion.json) counts candidates after chemistry and GIS terrain validation. Rows retain the survey, original sample ID, source file, SHA-256 and row locator.

- **United States.** Smith et al. (2013), [USGS Data Series 801](https://doi.org/10.3133/ds801), Appendix 2b. Use the 0–5 cm soil horizon, with positions supplied in WGS84. Convert reported magnesium, calcium, potassium, titanium and manganese to oxide-equivalent mass percentages. The source uses total or near-total ICP-based chemistry. Keep censored strings as missing. Silicon was unavailable in the table and silica remains missing. A quartz mineral percentage cannot supply total silica.
- **Ireland.** Geological Survey Ireland Tellus, [stream sediment archive](https://www.gsi.ie/data-and-maps/geochemistry/), release v1.1. Use the <150 µm fraction and the six published XRF oxide mass percentages. Exclude results below the stated detection limit or at the upper reporting limit. GIS transforms Irish National Grid coordinates from EPSG:29903 into WGS84. Contains Irish Public Sector Data (Geological Survey Ireland) licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence.
- **Topography and geology.** GIS applies the same NASADEM 5 km window, 500 m bins, 95% valid posting threshold, and shared slope metrics as in the parent release. Terrain rows retain the source windows and hashes. GLiM supplies geology at its native 0.5° resolution. Missing map values stay null. SPICE uses the same 24 dated Earth reference times and geometry as the parent release. See [methods and source links](ML.md).

Rebuild with `python build_earth_expansion.py` after installing the repository requirements plus `openpyxl`, `pyproj`, `rasterio`, `pyshp`, `shapely`, and `spiceypy`. Source files are checksum-pinned. The GIS processing cache is in `work/earth`. The browser app rebuilds with `python build_app.py`.

## Verification and source review

- Re-read both checksum-pinned chemistry files and reproduced every field of the 12,404 published expansion samples, including converted values, withheld values, locations, and row references. The snapshot contains 12,402 unique sites and 297,648 calculated SPICE scenario rows. Reproduced all 12,171 Earth GIS geology assignments from GLiM and all map assignments for the 137 Mars and 12 Moon profiles, including unmatched results. Compared original elevation window hashes, valid posting counts and all six terrain features for three sites per survey (20th, 50th and 80th percentiles of sorted site identifiers). The terrain replay was a sample of six sites.
- The supplementary [Irish bedrock map](https://www.gsi.ie/data-and-maps/bedrock/) joined all 7,648 Irish sites to one 1:100,000 polygon each. GIS projects WGS84 coordinates to Irish Transverse Mercator (EPSG:2157) and checks containment against the original polygons. [The sidecar](data/irish-bedrock.json) records the source archive hash, layer, polygon record index, unit ID, stratigraphy, lithology code, original name and description. Run `python build_irish_bedrock.py` after the expansion build. Results show this reference beside the scored map units. Contains Irish Public Sector Data (Geological Survey Ireland) licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence.

| [USGS DS-801](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ds801) and [USGS Geochemical Data Portal](https://alaska.usgs.gov/science/geology/geochem_portal/geochem_portal.html) | DS-801 already supplies US sites and covers the contiguous states. The wider portal offers more regions and materials. Select a survey, sample medium, preparation, digestion and analytic method before converting verified elemental totals to oxide equivalents. Measure or source total silica directly where the original method omits silicon. |
| [EarthChem / PetDB](https://search.earthchem.org/datatogo) and [NAVDAT](https://www.navdat.org/about) | Use for an explicit whole-rock comparison mode. Preserve the individual study and sample ID, measured panel, method, terrestrial collection location and license. Distinguish whole rock from loose soil or sediment, and land sites from seafloor or drill samples. Apply compositional total checks according to how each study reports moisture, volatile loss and iron. Laboratory totals support QC alongside that metadata. |
| [USAP-DC McMurdo soils](https://www.usap-dc.org/view/dataset/601684), [GeoMAP](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02152-9), [REMA](https://www.pgc.umn.edu/guides/stereo-derived-elevation-models/pgc-dem-products-arcticdem-rema-and-earthdem/) | GeoMAP and REMA can supply lithology and terrain for Antarctic sites. The examined USAP-DC McMurdo release describes water-track and dry-soil properties. Admission to the chemistry ranking still requires a coordinate-matched bulk oxide panel from an identified source. |
| [Copernicus DEM GLO-30 Public](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/) | Candidate for higher latitudes. It is a surface model that includes vegetation and structures, with gaps in the public 30 m tile set and a different grid layout from the current pipeline. Test GIS terrain metrics on overlapping locations and retain the exact product per row before combining sources. |
| [ESDAC LUCAS Topsoil](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/lucas-2009-topsoil-data) | Soil texture and spectra support separate test modes. The 2009 survey's agronomic chemistry lacks the bulk major-oxide panel used here. A spectral mode requires compatible wavelength, calibration and scale across bodies. |
| [NASA LOLA](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/lro/lola/), [Diviner](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/diviner.htm), [Mars THEMIS](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/odyssey/themis.html), [NASA POWER](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/) | Candidate layers for terrain-aware solar exposure, subsurface cold traps or thermal behavior. The sources measure different quantities. Keep zenith geometry, local horizon shadow, irradiance and thermal inertia as separate fields. For global Mars thermal inertia, [PDS TES maps](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mgs/tes-timap.html) are another documented product. |
